Live App URL (Hosted on qRaptor Platform)

The link to your deployed Agent-Native App. Should be publicly accessible or with credentials provided.

Required

Short Demo Video (2–5 mins)

A screen recording explaining what problem you're solving, how the app works, and how qRaptor helped build it. Upload to YouTube & Instagram tagging the qRaptor Page and share the link.

Project GitHub/GitLab Repository

Include the repo link if your app includes custom code. Mention which parts were built outside qRaptor.

Optional but Encouraged

LinkedIn Post Screenshot or Link

Share your build story on LinkedIn and tag @qraptor with #A2HackFest #GenAI #AgenticAI hashtags.

Credentials (If Needed)

Any test user accounts required to access the solution. Keep API keys safe for public sharing.

About A2HackFest

A2HackFest is your gateway into the world of Agentic AI—the future of application development. Participants will use qRaptor, our next-gen Agentic App Builder, to create intelligent applications powered by AI agents.

Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting your tech journey, qRaptor makes it easy to build apps through drag-and-drop interfaces, vibe-code mode with intuitive prompts, and one-click deployment.

Who Can Participate

Open to students, professionals, developers, and AI enthusiasts

Team leader must register on behalf of the team

Teams can have 1 to 3 members

No prior AI experience required — just bring your creativity!
